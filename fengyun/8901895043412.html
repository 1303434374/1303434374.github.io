User Instruction Manual

How to use

  • Fit the case onto the 11-inch Xiaomi Redmi Pad 2, carefully aligning each corner.
  • Use buttons, ports, and speakers through the precise cutouts without removing the case.
  • Keep the tablet's original appearance visible through the ultra-transparent TPU material.
  • Carry and use the tablet daily with added protection while keeping its slim, lightweight profile.

Safety and care

  • Confirm your device is the 2025 11.0-inch Xiaomi Redmi Pad 2 before fitting the case.
  • Do not fit the case onto other tablets; its openings are positioned for this specific model.
  • Press the flexible TPU around the corners gradually instead of stretching it unnecessarily during installation.
  • After fitting, check that the ports, buttons, and speakers remain fully uncovered and accessible.
