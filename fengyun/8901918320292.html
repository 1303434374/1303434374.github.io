Manual de utilizare

Cum se utilizeaza

  • Introduceți tableta Xiaomi Pad 7 sau Pad 7 Pro de 11,2 inchi în carcasa interioară, aliniind camera cu decupajul.
  • Pliați capacul frontal magnetic pentru a forma suportul și alegeți un unghi confortabil pentru utilizarea în modul landscape.
  • Deschideți capacul pentru activarea automată a tabletei și închideți-l pentru trecerea acesteia în modul repaus.

User Instruction Manual

How to use

  • Insert your 11.2-inch Xiaomi Pad 7 or Pad 7 Pro into the inner shell, aligning the camera with its cutout.
  • Fold the magnetic front cover into a stand and select a comfortable angle for landscape viewing or typing.
  • Open the cover to wake the tablet automatically, and close it to place the tablet into sleep mode.
  • Use the charging port, speakers, and buttons through their cutouts without removing the tablet from the protective shell.

Safety and care

  • Check that your device is the 2024 version of the 11.2-inch Xiaomi Pad 7 or Pad 7 Pro.
  • Do not force other tablet models into the shell; it is designed exclusively for the two specified models.
  • Before viewing or typing, check that the magnetic stand is stable and the tablet is positioned in landscape orientation.
  • Do not treat the hard shell and reinforced edges as a guarantee against damage; avoid drops and impacts.
