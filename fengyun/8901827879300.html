User Instruction Manual

How to use

  • Confirm your tablet is the 11-inch Xiaomi Redmi Pad 2 before fitting the case.
  • Seat the tablet in the rigid shell, aligning cutouts with cameras, ports, buttons, and speakers.
  • Fold the magnetic tri-fold cover to create a stable stand at your preferred viewing angle.
  • Use the adjustable stand for movies, video calls, or hands-free work on the tablet.

Safety and care

  • Do not force this case onto other Xiaomi models; it is designed exclusively for the 11-inch Redmi Pad 2.
  • Keep the cutouts free from dust to maintain unobstructed access to ports and speakers.
  • Avoid intentional drops; shock resistance reduces accidental damage but cannot prevent every possible impact damage.
  • Do not overbend the tri-fold cover, preserving its magnetic closure and adjustable stand function.
